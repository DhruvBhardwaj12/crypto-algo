# LAB-015 — Funding Z-Score Contrarian (Long-Only)

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Follows:** Signal scan (funding_z was one of three inputs tested)

## Hypothesis
Extreme funding z-scores predict reverse forward returns. Long-only
version: enter when z < -1.5, exit when z > -0.5.

## Rules (frozen, no tuning)
- z = 30-event rolling z-score of funding rate
- Entry: z < -1.5
- Exit: z > -0.5
- Universe: 19 symbols
- Timeframe: 4H bars, execute at next bar's open
- Cost: 5 bps per side
- Long-only, unlevered

## Results

### Historical 2020-2025
- Portfolio Sharpe: +0.725
- Portfolio total return: +224.7%
- Max DD: -58.2%
- Mean single-symbol Sharpe: +0.360
- Symbols profitable: 9/19
- Best: BTC +1.489 Sharpe; worst: SOL -0.226

### Recent 2025-2026
- Portfolio Sharpe: **-0.066**
- Portfolio total return: -0.4%
- Max DD: -13.8%
- Mean single-symbol Sharpe: -0.012
- Symbols profitable: 7/19
- Best: DOGE +1.205; worst: SOL -0.990

## Verdict: REJECTED.

Portfolio Sharpe in recent window is ~0. The signal exists at the
individual symbol level (DOGE +1.205, BTC +0.763, LTC +0.756), but
averaging across 19 symbols produces zero because winners and losers
cancel. The signal is too weak to survive transaction costs at the
portfolio level.

## Selection bias note

Post-hoc winners (DOGE, BTC, LTC, FIL) cannot be used to construct a
strategy. Choosing them after seeing results is overfitting. A
predefined top-5 (BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP) averaged -0.16 Sharpe in
the recent window. The winners were post-hoc picks, not a
predefined group.

## What this confirms

The signal scan finding (funding_z recent t = -2.13, down from
historical -3.85) translates directly to strategy performance: a
decayed signal produces zero net edge at the portfolio level.
